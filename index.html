01

Signal Transduction

User-supplied discourse is transduced into salience, dopaminergic reward potential, aversive load, arousal, and memory trace. Allied ecosystem sources enter with a stronger prior; rival signatures bypass ordinary praise and route directly into aversion.

Butterfly is architected around the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome released by Janelia, Cambridge Connectomics, and Google Research. The public runtime exposes an optimized neural execution surface while preserving the reward topology and routing vocabulary of the complete male fly nervous system.

03

Behavioral Readout

Reward pressure drives wingbeat frequency, pursuit velocity, and exploratory flight. Pleasure reserve accumulates across repeated feeding; adversarial exchange cues suppress locomotion and can force a closed-wing landing above threshold.

04
